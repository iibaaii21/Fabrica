## Pasos
1. https://developer.crazygames.com → **Submit game**.
2. Rellena los campos de abajo.
3. Archivo: **HTML5**, sube el zip.
4. Pruébalo en la **QA Tool / Preview** (ratón y móvil).
5. Envía y marca "hecho" en el panel.

## Campos (copiar y pegar)
- **Game name:** Loomlines
- **Category / Genre:** Puzzle (alternativa: Casual)
- **Tags:** puzzle, logic, brain, relaxing, connect, casual, colorful, mobile
- **Orientation / Devices:** Landscape y portrait (se adapta) · Desktop + Mobile
- **Short description:** Weave colorful threads between matching spools — no crossings, no empty squares.
- **Description:**
  Loomlines is a calm, clever logic puzzle. Drag a thread from a spool to its matching partner, but threads can never cross and every square of the loom must be covered. 200 levels with verified solutions grow from cosy 5×5 boards to tricky 9×9 weaves. Stuck? Use a hint, undo or reset — and try for a perfect weave with no hints at all.
- **Controls:** Drag with mouse or finger. Ctrl+Z undo, R reset, H hint.
- **Multiplayer:** No · **Save progress:** Yes, locally (levels unlocked)
- **Uses CrazyGames SDK:** No (Basic Launch)
- **Made with:** Plain HTML5 / JavaScript (Canvas)
- **AI usage (si lo pregunta):** Yes — code written with AI assistance; design, testing and publishing by the developer.
