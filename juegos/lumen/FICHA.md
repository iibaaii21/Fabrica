## Descripción (pegar tal cual)
**Hold to rise. Release to fall. Don't touch the glow.**

Guide a drifting spark of light through an endless neon cave that keeps getting faster and tighter. One button, instant restarts, and that "just one more run" feeling.

- ✨ Collect sparks to chain combos up to **x10**
- 🛡️ Grab rare **gold sparks** for a shield that saves you once
- 🔺 Crystal spikes appear as you go deeper
- 📱 Plays great on phone, tablet and desktop

**Controls:** hold mouse / touch / Space / ↑ to rise, release to fall. P to pause.
