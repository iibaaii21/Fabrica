## Pasos
1. Entra en https://developer.crazygames.com con tu cuenta.
2. Pulsa **Submit game** / **Add new game**.
3. Rellena los campos con lo de abajo (copia y pega).
4. En el archivo: tipo **HTML5**, sube el zip.
5. Usa la **QA Tool / Preview** del propio portal: comprueba que carga y se juega con ratón, teclado y móvil.
6. Envía. Marca en el panel "hecho".

## Campos (copiar y pegar)
- **Game name:** Lumen Drift
- **Category / Genre:** Arcade (alternativa: Casual)
- **Tags:** one button, endless, neon, arcade, casual, high score, skill, relaxing
- **Orientation / Devices:** Landscape · Desktop + Mobile (responde a ratón, toque y teclado)
- **Short description:** Hold to rise, release to fall — drift a spark of light through an endless neon cave.
- **Description:**
  Guide a drifting spark of light through an endless neon cave that keeps getting faster and tighter. One button, instant restarts and that "just one more run" feeling. Collect sparks to chain combos up to x10, grab rare gold sparks for a shield that saves you once, and dodge the crystal spikes that appear as you go deeper. How far can you drift?
- **Controls:** Hold left mouse button, touch, Space or Up arrow to rise; release to fall. P to pause.
- **Multiplayer:** No · **Save progress:** Best score saved locally
- **Uses CrazyGames SDK:** No (Basic Launch)
