## Pasos
1. https://developer.crazygames.com → **Submit game**.
2. Archivo: **HTML5**, sube el zip sin extraer.
3. Rellena los campos de abajo y sube imágenes y vídeos.
4. Pruébalo en la **QA Tool / Preview** (ratón y móvil) → envía.

## Campos (copiar y pegar)
- **Game name:** Lunavault
- **Category / Genre:** Casual (alternativa: Arcade)
- **Tags:** one button, tap, space, endless, arcade, casual, skill, mobile
- **Orientation / Devices:** Portrait preferred (works in landscape too) · Desktop + Mobile
- **Short description:** Tap to leap from moon to moon and climb as high as you can.
- **Description:**
  Lunavault is a one-tap space hopper. Your little star orbits a moon: tap at the right moment to leap off and land on the next one, higher and higher. Fly straight through a moon's centre for a PERFECT landing and chain combos for big bonus points. Grab glowing gems on the way, and watch out: moons spin faster, get smaller and start drifting sideways the higher you climb. Instant restarts, smooth on phone and desktop. How high can you vault?
- **Controls:** Click, tap, Space or Up arrow to leap. P to pause, M to mute.
- **Multiplayer:** No · **Save progress:** Best score saved locally
- **Uses CrazyGames SDK:** No (Basic Launch)
- **Made with:** Plain HTML5 / JavaScript (Canvas)
- **AI usage (si lo pregunta):** Yes — code made with AI assistance; design, testing and publishing by the developer.
